THAN QUIZ ASSISTANT — PHIEN BAN 0.2 (WINDOWS)

1. Cai Python 3.11+ tu python.org, chon Add Python to PATH.
2. Chay install.bat.
3. Cai Tesseract OCR Windows (UB Mannheim build), chon Vietnamese language data.
4. Chay start.bat.
5. Mo tab Kho dap an > Nhap PDF. PDF can co cau hoi va dap an duoc ghi ro (vi du: Cau 1: ... / A. ... / Dap an: A).
   PDF scan hinh anh chua duoc OCR trong chuc nang nhap PDF; can chuyen thanh PDF co text truoc.
   Xem va xac nhan ban xem truoc truoc khi luu; kiem tra lai ket qua nhap.
6. Mo vbtm-tm. Trong tab Cai dat, nhap toa do vung cau hoi (X,Y,rong,cao).
7. Nhan F8 de chup/nhan dien cau hoi; hoac dan van ban truc tiep de doi chieu.

LUU Y QUAN TRONG:
- Phien ban 0.1 hoat dong voi OCR, kho dap an, tim dap an, hien thi ket qua va che do lap thu cong.
- Nut Auto Mode la BAN THU NGHIEM: chua tu click lua chon. Phai hieu chinh va kiem thu toa do tren giao dien thuc te truoc khi bat tu dong click. Khong tu nop bai.
- OCR tieng Viet co the sai, nhat la cac nut radio/checkbox khong co ky tu OCR ro rang.
- Du lieu luu tai %APPDATA%\ThanQuizAssistant\questions.json.
- Chi su dung tu dong hoa khi duoc phep trong bai luyen tap.
